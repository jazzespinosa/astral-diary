Our Mission

A serene space where your daily reflections live among the stars. We believe everyone deserves a private, beautiful sanctuary to chronicle their journey through the cosmos of life — free from noise and distraction.

Security & Privacy

Your entries are encrypted on your device and securely stored on our servers. Our 2-part key system ensures your diary remains private and accessible across all your devices without the risk of losing access.

Features

  • Focused writing space with option to attach images
  • Custom themed calendar to keep track of important dates
  • 3D moon page and stars that shows random entries from your diary
  • Strong encryption with 2-part key system
